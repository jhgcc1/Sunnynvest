the perfect tool for financial analysis of investments in solar power plants.

impress your customer with an advanced financial report of potential investments in photovoltaic energy

For a robust investment analysis and feasibility study, various tools can be used. The most common financial tools such as cash flow, intern return rate, modified intern return rate, net present value, equivalent annual annuity, traditional payback, discounted payback and Pareto chart are considered a must regarding to any kind of investment analysis.

In addition to the traditional financial tools, statistics tools such as time series regressions, probability theory and Monte Carlo simulation together with sensitive analysis can be used to predict returns and measure risks. Sunnynvest makes inferences in case you decide to postpone when you do the investment, it considers that the price of photovoltaic energy has been changing with time, consequently giving a better overview of how an investment will perform if postponements happens. Sunnynvest makes use of all this mentioned tools and provides a full report generated by a Artificial Inteligence engine.


Sunnynvest stores all your project's simulation in a database that way any of your projects can be reloaded from the profile page.to load a project, log in your account, click on profile tab and then choos your project on the dropdown menu shown in your profile followed by a click on the button load project.




Sunnynvest is also available in desktop version, it was firstly called Solinvest and can be considered the version 1 of the software, if your interested in the desktop version dont hesitate to contact us. The desktop version includes all the analasis of the web version but it has less options of porbability distributions to choose in case of stochastic inputs.
