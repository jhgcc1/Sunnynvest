For a robust investment analysis and feasibility study, various tools can be used. The most common financial tools such as cash flow, intern return rate, modified intern return rate, net present value, equivalent annual annuity, profitability index, traditional payback, discounted payback and Pareto chart are considered a must regarding to any kind of investment analysis.

In addition to the traditional financial tools, statistics tools such as time series regressions, probability theory and Monte Carlo simulation together with sensitive analysis can be used to predict returns, in case you decide to postpone when you do the investment considering that the price of photovoltaic energy has been changing with time, and measure risks, consequently giving a better overview of how an investment will perform. Sunnynvest makes use of all this tools and provides a full report generated by a Artificial Inteligence engine.

Sunnynvest stores all your project's simulation in a database that way any of your projects can be reloaded from the profile page.to load a project, log in your account, click on profile tab and then choos your project on the dropdown menu shown in your profile followed by a click on the button load project.
