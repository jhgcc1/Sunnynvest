For a robust investment analysis and feasibility study, various tools can be used. The most common financial tools such as cash flow, intern return rate, modified intern return rate, net present value, equivalent annual annuity, traditional payback, discounted payback and Pareto chart are considered a must regarding to any kind of investment analysis.

In addition to the traditional financial tools, statistics tools such as time series regressions, probability theory and Monte Carlo simulation together with sensitive analysis can be used to predict returns and measure risks. Sunnynvest makes use of all this mentioned tools and provides a full report generated by a Artificial Inteligence engine.



Why Sunnynvest?


With the growing demand for solar energy, motivated mainly by the movement against the use of fossil fuels, thousands of entrepreneurs venture into this field to sell photovoltaic energy systems. To make life easier for companies in the industry, software for projects and simulation of energy production were created and disseminated in the market. However, when doing research with several companies in the solar panel sales industry, it was noted that the software on the market did not provide sufficiently good financial economic reports, and that most companies in the industry were choosing to create spreadsheets demonstrating the economic benefits investment in solar energy.

As each company creates their own spreadsheet, it was possible to notice a lack of standard in the financial analysis, causing confusion, since disparities in the economic returns between similar projects often occurred, compromising the liability of the investment. Some photovoltaic systems sellers were providing their customer economic viability projects with gross errors, causing extreme distrust for potential buyers. Seeing this opportunity and willing to solve this problem in the market, Sunnynvest was created. Our software makes a robust financial and economic analysis, using concepts of artificial intelligence and statistics, taking into account all the smallest details that impact the return on investment in photovoltaic energy. The software, in addition to providing several financial indicators, also performs a stochastic analysis of the investment through Monte Carlo simulation and sensitivity analysis, that way the investor can know the risks and probabilities of success of the project, thus having greater confidence at the investment. Along with all the stochastic risk analysis, Sunnynvest also predicts how the investment would perform if it were to be postponed for a few years. The forecast is made through an exponential regression based on past data on the price of photovoltaic solar energy. There is no software on the market that makes an investment analysis as complete, robust and reliable as Sunnynvest.
